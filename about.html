About Feuern Outsourcing

A remote back office team for property preservation contractors.

Who we are

Feuern Outsourcing is a remote back office team working US time zones. We have processed more than 20,000 property preservation work orders since 2020, for contractors across ten states, from solo owner operators to multi crew operations.

We started because we kept seeing the same thing: good field contractors losing their nights to portal updates, bid writing, and invoice corrections. The field work was never the problem. The processing was.

So we built a service around it. Damage focused bid writing. Photo review against real portal QC requirements. Invoicing matched to allowables. Completion packages that go in clean the first time. And real time support for crews still on site.

Our background combines computer science education with years of hands on preservation processing across national platforms. Clients stay for years, and most new clients arrive by referral.

You handle the field work and photos. We handle the processing.

Founded by Aminol Adud in 2022.
