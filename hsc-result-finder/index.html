While running, the search continues on the server when you switch apps. It may pause for a match or board restriction. To reopen after closing this tab, save your private recovery link; anyone with the link can see and control this search. Progress expires after 7 days.

If the search stops

For a temporary board error such as HTTP 520, the tool tries the same roll twice automatically. If it still stops, wait and tap Retry current roll to continue without losing checked results. Do not bypass verification or access restrictions.

How this independent tool works & privacy

The online number is an estimate of browser tabs active in the last two minutes, not verified people. A random tab ID and recent activity time are stored briefly to count tabs and arrange search turns. You can optionally send a hello with a name or nickname; only the site owner can see it. A hello is stored with its random tab ID to limit repeats, and both are deleted after 30 days. An anonymous hello has no name. Short-lived connection information helps prevent abuse. The hosting and database providers, and the board, receive the connection information needed to serve requests. When a college is loaded, its groups are queued for background collection independently of your search, subject to board and hosting limits. Active searches take priority. Board-verified institute lists and student results are stored until the owner deletes them and can be reused by other visitors; backups may retain copies until removed, and results may become outdated unless refreshed. If a refresh finds no result, an older saved copy is hidden from normal reuse but retained for the owner to delete. Uploaded PDFs are not published as verified lists. Private search names, progress and checked results are scheduled to expire after 7 days; cleanup can be delayed by outages. The site owner and hosting provider may have access to stored data. Anyone with a private recovery link can access that search. Uploaded PDFs stay in your browser, but extracted rolls are sent to the server for the private background search.
