New Vendor Checklist

Ten steps from zero to your first work orders in the mortgage field services industry.

The ten steps

1. Register the business

LLC, EIN, business bank account. This is 1099 work.

2. Get insured

$1M general liability + errors & omissions. Workers' comp, or a zero-employee exemption on file.

3. Clear the background check

ABC# / Shield Hub. An IC01 rating is ideal.

4. Define your coverage area

2 to 3 counties, roughly a 30 to 40 mile radius you can serve reliably.

5. Set up the field kit

Tools for your services, reliable vehicle, camera or phone with the date and time set correctly.

6. Apply to five or more nationals

Do not wait for one answer. Your income should never depend on one company's volume.

7. Complete portal training

When approved (typically a few weeks to a few months), treat training as part of the job. Every portal is different.

8. Start with inspections and maintenance

Earn repair and rehab work with a clean track record.

9. Document everything

Before, during, after. Every listed view. Correct datestamps. Nothing readable that should not be.

10. Consider back-office support early

The processing workload grows with every order you take. A processing partner keeps your evenings yours.

Where to apply (start here)

Safeguard · MCS · Five Brothers (part of MCS) · ServiceLink · Cyprexx · Altisource · NFR · A2Z · Guardian · VRM · United · First Allegiance · Brookstone · Northsight

Other doors in: HUD's contractor registry for FHA work, Fannie Mae's REOTrans, and Freddie Mac's HomeSteps for REO disposition. If you are a crew rather than a business owner, subcontract for a regional vendor company first, that is the fastest route in.
