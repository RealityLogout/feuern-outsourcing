Scope of work (what, where, how much)

Describe the exact work, item by item, with location in the property and approximate dimensions or quantities. Write it so a reviewer who has never seen the property can approve it without asking a question.

Justification

Why this work is needed and why this price: cause of damage (if visible), access constraints, materials, labor, disposal fees, anything that explains the numbers.
Photo references: photo attached to this bid.
Three rules for bids that get approved: (1) Match the scope to the photos, exactly. (2) Use standard line-item wording, Bluebook-compliant where the client requires it. (3) Submit before the work starts, never after. Work above the allowable without an approved bid usually means you absorb the difference.
