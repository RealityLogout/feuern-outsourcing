The Mortgage Field Services Industry, One Page

Who does what, and where the money flows. Print this and keep it with your field kit.

1. Lender / Servicer / Investor (the client)

Banks, credit unions, mortgage servicers, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA/HUD, VA. They hold or service the loan, pay for the field work, and publish the rules, especially allowable pricing.

↓

2. National field service company (the national)

Safeguard · MCS · Five Brothers (part of MCS) · ServiceLink · Cyprexx · Altisource · NFR · A2Z · Guardian · VRM · United · First Allegiance · Brookstone · Northsight. They hold the client relationships and run the vendor networks and portals.

↓

3. Regional vendor company (the middle layer)

Hold vendor contracts with several nationals and manage the local crews. Examples: Mary B. and Edgard V. This is the layer most new contractors work with first.

↓

4. Field vendor / Crew (the boots on the ground)

Contractors, handymen, lawn crews, cleanout crews, laborers. They inspect, secure, maintain, and repair the properties. Paid per work order, as 1099 independent contractors.

Runs alongside the whole chain: the back-office processor

Work order processing and portal updates, bid writing, photo review and labeling, PCRs, invoicing, completion packages, QC, and reporting. That is what Feuern Outsourcing does for U.S. preservation contractors, so the vendor's evenings stay free.

Money flow: Lender/servicer → national → regional vendor → field crew. It is 1099 work (W-9 on file). Pricing is allowable-driven; work above the allowable needs an approved bid. No public price lists, ever.
