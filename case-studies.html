From Photos to Clear Bid Scope

The original bid material covered multiple repair and safety tasks. For public use, client-specific fields, dollar amounts, and estimator identifiers are removed — the structure below is what actually goes into the portal.

Task Scope prepared from field evidence
Drywall / Ceiling Repair Repair and seal damaged wall and ceiling surfaces; includes preparation and finish work.
Outlet-Cover Replacement Replace missing or damaged cover plates and secure the affected electrical opening.
Tile Replacement Remove and replace damaged floor tiles creating a trip hazard; haul away debris.
Exposed-Wire Securing Secure loose or exposed wiring at documented interior and exterior locations.
