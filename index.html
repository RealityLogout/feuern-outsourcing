How Our Processing Workflow Works

From intake to a recorded submission, eight steps, every time.

01

Receive & Confirm

Receive the order and confirm the correct property or work order.

02

Preflight Review

Check instructions, photos, notes and required evidence before processing.

03

Organize Evidence

Sort photos and documents by order, task, bid and portal section.

04

Process & Prepare

Complete PCRs, prepare scopes, select line items and match evidence.

05

Update Portal

Enter photos, bids, notes, completed work and invoice information.

06

QC Review

Check requirements, task placement, photos, bids, invoices and deadlines.

07

Client Review

Send a summary for approval when the client requests a review.

08

Submit & Record

Submit when authorized and record the order for follow up.

Fire Hazard / Tree Removal $42,000

Dead & Fire Burned Tree Removal

Remove (10) dead and fire burned severe fire hazard large trees and lots of limbs or wooden tree pieces approx. 60 limbs that have fallen down and are in the way of accessing the property as they are dangerous and in the way of the driveway access to the property. There are also separate tree stumps that need to be grinded and removed. That will required stump grinder to rent with other landscaping tools like chainsaw, woodchipper, approximately 200 cyds of debris will be generated removing trees.

Apply Bleach and Kilz $373.03

Water Damage Bid

Apply a bleach and water mixture, then prime/seal with a stain blocker (Kilz type product), 2 coats, under the kitchen sink cabinet around 3x5 sqft. Area has water damage due to possible prior water seepage and requires treatment to help prevent mold regrowth. Includes spray and wipe down, along with gloves and surgical mask for safety protection.

Custom Garage Door $6,788.82

Custom Overhead Garage Door Replacement

Remove temporary boarding from a single car garage door custom size. Remove the existing damaged overhead garage door and replace it with a new custom overhead garage door with roller, spring and any necessary hardware. The existing door is broken beyond repair and has been temporarily boarded for security; the boarding must be removed prior to door removal.

Propane Tank $1,800

Above Ground Propane Tank Removal

Remove (1) ABOVE GROUND PROPANE TANK & DISPOSE, need to remove safely and dispose (1) 250 gallon propane tank located near fence line outside of the property, need to verify disconnection of it to prevent safety hazard, transport and dispose to damp properly, in order to prevent health hazard.

Pool Securing $3,085.96

In Ground Pool Cover Installation

Install a pool cover on an in ground swimming pool approximately 35 ft x 18 ft to secure the area and prevent a fall hazard. Installation will be completed after draining the pool and performing the initial shock and treatment.

Yard Structure Removal $4,350.95

Detached Outbuilding / Coop Structure Removal

Demolish and remove (3) detached outbuilding/coop structures located in rear yard area. Structures are deteriorated and not permanent construction, creating safety hazard, pest harborage risk, and obstruction to yard maintenance. Bid includes full dismantling of all three coop structures including wood framing, wire mesh/cage materials, tarp coverings and generated debris removal.
