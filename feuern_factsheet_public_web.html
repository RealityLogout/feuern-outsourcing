Who We Are

Feuern Outsourcing is a remote back-office company headquartered in Dhaka, Bangladesh, supporting US property preservation and mortgage field service contractors. We handle everything after the field visit — you handle the field work and photos, we handle the processing — so you can stay in the field and take on more volume.

Services

Work Order Processing & Portal Updates
End-to-end processing across national and regional vendor portals: order intake and tracking, completion entries, note documentation, photo upload, invoice submission, status updates, and reopen/dispute handling with backcharge prevention review.
Bid & Proposition Writing
Approval-ready, damage-focused bids built from field photos and prior order history — clear scope, correct quantities, and cost justification tied to visible damage. Bluebook-compliant line items where required. Also available standalone, with no portal access needed.
Photo Review, Labeling & QC
Photo review, labeling, grass-cut archive organization, correct seasonal photo reuse, datestamp adjustments, and letterhead generation. Every photo set is checked against portal QC requirements before submission.
PCR, Invoicing & Completion Packages
Property condition reports completed from photos and order data. Line-item invoices prepared against allowables and bid approvals. Full completion packages — photos, notes, invoices, documentation — assembled submission-ready.
Vendor Management & Reporting
Excel-based order tracking, work order summary reports, structured client communication, and contractor coordination — included with processing, not sold separately. Real-time field support: crews send photos from the property and we verify every work order instruction and requirement is covered before they leave.
